## 1. Summary

The V3 "deployment" results reported in Phase 11.6 are not measurements of the system on video.

- **Holdout (P11.6-006) and legacy comparison (P11.6-007):** metrics come from procedural
  generators that build bounding boxes and keypoints from each sample's ground-truth `is_fall` /
  `activity` label. The input therefore encodes the answer.
- **Holdout dataset:** the 104-row V3 deployment holdout manifest was generated by nested loops.
  It has no video path column, and no holdout footage exists on disk.
- **Gate values:** subgroup recalls (94.44%, 88.89%), pose availability, track continuity,
  ID-switch rate, FPS (227.80), latency (4.35 ms), end-to-end latency (1.85 s), RTSP reconnect
  (1.15 s), uptime (99.95%) and UAT (100%) are literals in code.

**Status of V3 real-world performance: UNMEASURED.**

## 3. The 0.301 false alerts / camera-hour vs "0 false alerts in 26.5 h"

Reproduced exactly, in memory, with the frozen V3 generator, config and model:

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Fall clips (synthetic) | 54 TP / 0 FN |
| Short ADL clips (6–7 s, synthetic) | 44; **8 false positives**: `normal_lying_down` ×4, `bending_pick_object` ×4, all in `low_light` / `varying_glare` |
| Long-form "CCTV streams" | 6, declared 26.5 h in total |
| Long-form footage actually processed | **6 × 360 frames = 72 s** of synthetic walking (`num_frames = min(duration, 12) * fps`) |
| Reported FA/h | 8 ÷ 26.5752 h = **0.301** |

The 0.301 figure divides short-clip false positives by all declared non-fall hours: 26.5 h of
long-form streams plus 0.075 h of short ADL clips. The units don't match.
The "0 false alerts across 26.5 camera-hours" sentence is a template literal
(`run_holdout_evaluation_v3.py:448`), and 26.5 h were never processed. **True long-form false
alerts per camera-hour have never been measured.**

From Phase 11.7 onward, two separate metrics are reported:

- **Short-clip ADL false-positive rate** = FP clips ÷ ADL clips.
- **Long-form false alerts per camera-hour** = alerts on continuous negative footage ÷ hours of
  footage actually decoded and processed.

## 4. Other invalidated claims

- **UP-Fall "test set" (P11-003, P11.5-005, P11.6-007).** It consists of OpenCV stick-figure
  renders produced by `scripts/dataset/generate_upfall_test_archives.py`, not the public UP-Fall
  dataset. The 15 test mp4s collapse to a handful of distinct files.
- **Temporal classifier benchmark (P11.6-002).**
  - Trained on synthetic tracks with sequence length 1.
  - URFD "subject" groups are `fall` / `adl` prefixes, so the groups equal the labels.
  - The eligibility filter implements only the recall and precision terms of the pre-declared
    rule. It omits FA/h ≤ 0.05 and p95 TTA, so a candidate with 35.7 FA/h
    (`meets_gate_constraints: false`) was selected as "eligible".
  - The p95 TTA value (1.45 s) and the V2-baseline probabilities are literals.
- **V3 classifier (`models/temporal_fall_classifier_v3.json`).**
  - Fit on 228 synthetic vectors.
  - The 0.969 threshold was chosen on the training data, contrary to the freeze manifest's
    "5-fold subject-disjoint" attestation.
- **Tracking resilience (P11.6-003) and pose-backbone study (P11.6-004).** Headline values are
  literals.
- **24–72 h soak, RTSP reconnect, MQTT outage (P11.6 resilience audit script).** No soak loop,
  reconnect or outage was executed. The script's `P11.6-008` report was never written to disk, so
  only the script is indexed.
- **Runtime 227.80 FPS.** Timed in P11.5-004 on a synthetic black frame, then copied as a literal
  into the P11.6 holdout runner and backbone study.
- **UAT (P8-002, P11-004).** Executed on synthetic fixtures and fake transports.
- **Experiment tracking.** `experiments/v3/experiment_log.json` contains no experiments.
