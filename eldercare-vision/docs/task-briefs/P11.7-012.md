# Task Brief: P11.7-012 — False-Alert Suppressor on Complex ADLs

## 1. Context & Objectives
In unconstrained residential and assisted-living environments, complex activities of daily living (ADLs) frequently produce motion profiles that resemble falls to simple heuristic or threshold detectors:
1. **Forward Bending / Tying Shoes / Picking Up Objects:** Head and torso descend rapidly, and torso angle drops from vertical (~85°) to near-horizontal (~25°), yet feet/ankles remain stationary and deceleration is controlled without floor impact.
2. **Sitting Down Rapidly (Sofa / Chair / Wheelchair):** Aspect ratio and height decrease, but hip center remains elevated above the floor plane (>0.25h above ground) on furniture.
3. **Intentional Reclining / Bed Transitions:** Posture transitions from upright to horizontal without free-fall acceleration spikes, settling into a stable rest position.

This task implements a dedicated heuristic and calibrated secondary suppression gate (`ADLFalseAlertSuppressor`) to eliminate false alarms on complex ADLs while ensuring genuine falls remain 100% uninhibited.

---

## 2. Technical Scope
- **`src/eldercare/fall_engine/suppression/adl_suppressor.py`:**
  - `SuppressionReason`: Categorical reasons (`CONTROLLED_BENDING`, `CONTROLLED_SITTING`, `INTENTIONAL_RECLINING`, `CLASSIFIER_VETO`, `ELEVATED_HIP_SURFACE`, `NONE`).
  - `ADLSuppressionConfig`: Thresholds for ankle stability, hip elevation, and calibrated classifier veto ($\tau_{\text{veto}} = 0.55$).
  - `ADLFalseAlertSuppressor`: Evaluates kinematics, keypoint trajectories, and classifier probability to emit `SuppressionResult`.
- **State Machine Integration (`TrackFallStateMachineV3`):**
  - Integrated into down confirmation phase to safely abort candidate falls triggered by complex ADLs before `FALL_CONFIRMED` event emission.
- **Verification:**
  - Comprehensive unit test suite (`tests/unit/test_v4_adl_suppression.py`).
