# P11-005 — Task Brief: Final Metrics Consolidation

## Objective
Programmatically consolidate and report all final evaluation metrics from Phase 11 artifacts across URFD, UP-Fall, and Local UAT, presenting separate per-dataset metrics, combined video summaries, confusion matrices, false-alert frequencies, time-to-alert latency, and explicit comparison against predefined portfolio targets without modifying detector parameters.

## Scope
1. **Programmatic Consolidation**:
   - Ingest `docs/reports/P11-002-urfd-raw-evaluation.json`, `docs/reports/P11-003-upfall-raw-evaluation.json`, and `docs/reports/P11-004-uat-report.md`.
   - Calculate TP, FP, TN, FN, Accuracy, Precision, Recall, Specificity, F1-Score, and false alerts per hour.
2. **Confusion Matrix Generation**:
   - Construct confusion matrices for URFD (28 seqs), UP-Fall (15 seqs), and combined video benchmarks (43 seqs).
3. **Latency & Time-to-Alert**:
   - Compile authoritative TTA measurements on valid onset-annotated ground truth.
4. **Target Comparison**:
   - Transparently compare measured empirical results against targets.
5. **Documentation**:
   - Generate `docs/reports/P11-005-final-metrics-report.md` and `docs/reports/P11-005-final-metrics.json`.
   - Complete `docs/task-reports/P11-005.md` and `docs/reviews/P11-005-review.md`.

## Constraints & Invariants
- Zero post-hoc threshold tuning or model retraining.
- Target misses are reported faithfully as empirical baseline findings.
