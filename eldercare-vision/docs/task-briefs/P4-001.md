# P4-001 — Synthetic pose/track fixtures

## Purpose
Create deterministic synthetic normal and fall pose/track sequences required by the Temporal Fall Engine tests. Reuses the existing Phase 2/Phase 3 domain contracts (`Keypoint`, `PersonPose`, `PoseFrame`, `TrackedPerson`, `TrackedFrame`, `TrackObservation`, `TrackHistory`) to provide pure, deterministic, CPU-safe test fixtures covering normal activities of daily living (walking, sitting, bending, slow lie-down) and fall scenarios (forward fall, backward fall, sideways fall, fall with recovery, partial keypoint occlusion, multi-person sequences).

## Requirements
- Reuse existing domain models (`Keypoint`, `PersonPose`, `PoseFrame`, `TrackedPerson`, `TrackedFrame`, `TrackObservation`, `TrackHistory`) rather than parallel ad-hoc representations.
- Pure and deterministic: identical calls yield identical instances/values; zero reliance on random seeds, clocks, or sleep calls.
- Scenario coverage:
  1. **Standing / Walking:** Constant upright posture, high height-to-width ratio (> 1.5), near-vertical torso orientation (~80°–90°), minimal vertical velocity.
  2. **Sitting Down:** Smooth, gradual descent over ~1.0–1.5s, upright/slight recline torso, reduced height-to-width ratio (~1.0–1.2), stable post-sit posture.
  3. **Bending / Picking Up Object:** Transient forward torso tilt (angle drops to ~30°–45°) for ~0.5–1.0s, followed by rapid recovery to upright stance.
  4. **Gentle Lying Down:** Slow, deliberate transition to horizontal posture over ~3.0–4.0s with low peak vertical velocity.
  5. **Rapid Fall Sequences:** Upright pre-fall phase -> rapid descent/velocity spike over ~0.3–0.5s with rapid torso rotation (~85° -> ~10°) and aspect ratio collapse (< 0.7) -> sustained post-fall low/horizontal immobility phase (forward, backward, and sideways variations).
  6. **Fall with Recovery:** Fall sequence followed by active posture recovery back to standing/seated within a few seconds.
  7. **Missing / Degraded Keypoints:** Explicit partial occlusion (e.g., lower limbs occluded by bed/furniture) with `present=False`, coordinates `None`, and lower confidences.
  8. **Multi-Person Sequences:** Concurrent independent tracks (e.g., Person 1 walking normally while Person 2 falls) to validate multi-track temporal isolation.
- Quality & safety:
  - CPU-safe, fast (< 1s execution).
  - No framework dependencies at import time (no torch, ultralytics, cv2).
  - No external downloads or real video files.
  - Comprehensive unit test coverage in `tests/unit/test_synthetic_fixtures.py`.
