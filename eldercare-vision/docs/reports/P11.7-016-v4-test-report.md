## 4. Key Takeaways & Transition to P11.7-017
- The V4 pipeline delivers consistent sub-second detection on fast kinetic falls (Median TTA = 0.533s).
- False positives on low-posture ADLs (lying on couch, tying shoes on floor) and false negatives on subtle slips identify the remaining edge-case failure modes to be cataloged in P11.7-017.
