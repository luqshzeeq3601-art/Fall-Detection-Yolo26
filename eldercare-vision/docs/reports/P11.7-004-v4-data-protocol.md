# P11.7-004 — Specification: V4 Real-World Data Protocol & Split Isolation Governance

**Status:** APPROVED & BINDING  
**Effective Date:** 2026-09-25  
**Scope:** All dataset acquisition, annotation, partitioning, and model training in Phase 11.7.

---

## 1. Safety, Privacy & Informed Consent Framework

### 1.1 Non-Negotiable Human Safety Directive
- **Strict Ban on Unsafe Falls:** Under no circumstances may any person be instructed or permitted to perform an unpadded or unprotected fall on hard surfaces.
- **Safety Standard:** Any physical fall simulations conducted for local data acquisition must utilize certified high-density gymnastic crash mats ($\ge 20\text{ cm}$ thickness), staged by trained individuals, or utilize passive non-hazardous actions (e.g., rolling gently from bed/chair to floor, sliding down from wall).

### 1.2 Privacy & Informed Consent Protocol
- **Participant Consent:** Every human subject appearing in locally recorded footage must execute an informed consent release granting permission for research and algorithm training.
- **PII Protection & De-Identification:** For non-public environments, facial blurring and removal of identifiable personal items (documents, photos) is mandatory before indexing.
- **Storage Isolation:** Raw unblurred footage must be stored in access-controlled encrypted volumes (`datasets/raw/local_secure/`) with only anonymized keypoints or blurred frames exported to training sets.

## 2. Licensing & Dataset Origin Rules

### 2.1 Permitted Public Datasets
- **UR Fall Detection (URFD):** Licensed under **CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0**. Permitted exclusively for research benchmark evaluation and non-commercial model development.
- **Genuine Public Repositories:** Any external video corpus must have its original licensing terms, author attribution, and source URL cryptographically logged.

### 2.2 Strict Rejection of Synthetic Renders as Real Evidence
- **Quarantine Enforcement:** Procedurally generated stick-figure animations (such as the legacy OpenCV renders created for UP-Fall) and synthetic label-derived sequences are strictly barred from being classified as `deployment_evidence=true`.
- **Definition of Real Evidence:** Deployment evidence requires photons captured by a camera sensor through optical lenses, decoded from a video container into discrete pixel matrices.

## 3. Annotation Schema & Quality Assurance Contract

### 3.1 17-Keypoint Standard (COCO Compatible)
Annotations must conform to the 17-keypoint standard compatible with YOLO26s-Pose:
`[nose, left_eye, right_eye, left_ear, right_ear, left_shoulder, right_shoulder, left_elbow, right_elbow, left_wrist, right_wrist, left_hip, right_hip, left_knee, right_knee, left_ankle, right_ankle]`.

Keypoint visibility:
- `v=0`: Not labeled / out of frame.
- `v=1`: Labeled but occluded.
- `v=2`: Labeled and visible with high confidence.

### 3.2 Event Temporal Annotations
Each video sequence must possess frame-indexed ground-truth intervals:
1. `fall_start_frame`: First frame where rapid downwards loss of balance commences.
2. `fall_end_frame`: Frame where downwards vertical kinetic descent terminates and body impacts floor.
3. `lying_start_frame`: Frame where sustained stationary ground-level posture is reached.
4. `recovery_frame`: Optional frame where person attempts or achieves standing/sitting posture.

## 4. Partitioning & Anti-Leakage Invariants

### 4.1 Strict Subject-Disjoint Partitioning
- **Zero Subject Overlap:** $\text{Subjects}(\text{Train}) \cap \text{Subjects}(\text{Dev}) = \emptyset$, $\text{Subjects}(\text{Train}) \cap \text{Subjects}(\text{Holdout}) = \emptyset$, and $\text{Subjects}(\text{Dev}) \cap \text{Subjects}(\text{Holdout}) = \emptyset$.
- Violating this invariant immediately halts execution with a `SplitLeakageError`.

### 4.2 Zero Hash Duplication
- Every video file and extracted feature representation must have a unique SHA-256 digest. No identical video may appear in multiple splits under different identifiers.

### 4.3 Programmatic Holdout Isolation
- Training and hyperparameter tuning routines must invoke `DatasetSplitGuard.enforce_training_isolation(split)`.
- Attempting to load rows marked as `holdout` or `test` during model training raises a `HoldoutAccessError`.
- Final holdout evaluation may be executed **strictly once** after the candidate model, hyperparameters, and feature schemas are frozen.
