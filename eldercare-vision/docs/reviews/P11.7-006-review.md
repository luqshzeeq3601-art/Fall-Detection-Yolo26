## 2. Review Checklist & Findings

### Axis 1: Correctness of Ingestion & Optical Authenticity
- **Optical Authenticity Validator:** Evaluated `OpticalAuthenticityValidator.validate_video()` on both genuine URFD video and synthetic OpenCV stick figures. Verified that authentic optical video achieves high unique colors ($> 2,800$), high Laplacian texture variance ($> 600$), and realistic color standard deviation, whereas synthetic stick figures are detected and rejected (`score < 0.5`, `is_optical_capture=False`).
- **Deployment Evidence Enforcement:** Verified that `deployment_evidence` is strictly gated on `is_optical_capture`. Synthetic artifacts cannot be classified as `deployment_evidence=true`.
- **Finding:** PASS. Meets Section 2.2 of the V4 Data Protocol.

### Axis 2: Split Isolation & Anti-Leakage Governance
- **Subject Disjointness:** Confirmed that the development split comprises 42 sequences across 6 unique subjects (`subj-01` through `subj-06`), and the held-out test split comprises 28 sequences across 4 unique subjects (`subj-07` through `subj-10`).
- **Zero Subject Overlap:** Confirmed that $\text{Subjects}(\text{Dev}) \cap \text{Subjects}(\text{Test}) = \emptyset$ via `DatasetSplitGuard.verify_partitions`.
- **Zero Hash Duplication:** Confirmed that all 70 videos possess unique SHA-256 digests with zero cross-split hash collisions.
- **Holdout Isolation:** Confirmed that `DatasetSplitGuard.enforce_training_isolation()` blocks any training script from accessing the test/holdout split, raising an uncatchable `HoldoutAccessError`.
- **Finding:** PASS. Meets Section 4 of the V4 Data Protocol.

### Axis 3: Ground-Truth Temporal Annotations
- **Temporal Alignment:** Confirmed that `DatasetIngestionEngine.parse_urfd_ground_truth()` parses `urfall-cam0-falls.csv` accurately, extracting `fall_start_frame`, `fall_end_frame`, and `lying_start_frame` across all 30 fall sequences.
- **Verification:** Unit tests confirm that `fall_start_frame <= fall_end_frame` for 100% of fall sequences.
- **Finding:** PASS. Meets Section 3.2 of the V4 Data Protocol.

### Axis 4: Manifest Cryptographic Integrity & Tamper Detection
- **Manifest Parity:** Confirmed that `v4_multi_source_manifest.json` and `v4_multi_source_manifest.csv` match exactly in record count (70) and fields.
- **Tamper Verification:** Confirmed that `DatasetIngestionEngine.verify_manifest_integrity()` checks all 70 videos against disk and raises `SplitLeakageError` when a hash mismatch or corrupted file is detected.
- **Quarantine Check:** Confirmed that all 50 quarantined artifacts in `tests/unit/test_evidence_quarantine.py` pass 100% byte-matched verification with exact sha256 checksum locks.
- **Finding:** PASS.
