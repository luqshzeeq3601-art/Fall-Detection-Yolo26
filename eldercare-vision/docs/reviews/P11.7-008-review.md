## 2. Review Checklist & Findings

### Axis 1: Strict Partition Isolation & Anti-Leakage Verification
- **Isolation Enforcement:** Audited `DatasetSplitGuard.enforce_training_isolation("dev")` at startup of `train_v4_classifier.py`. Confirmed that any attempt to load test/holdout sequences (`subj-07` through `subj-10`) raises a fatal `HoldoutAccessError`.
- **Subject Check:** Verified that all 8,735 cached temporal feature samples in `datasets/cache/v4_dev_features.npz` originate exclusively from the 6 development subjects (`subj-01` to `subj-06`). Zero test samples or subjects are present.
- **Finding:** PASS. Zero data leakage into training or cross-validation.

### Axis 2: Real-World Data Authenticity & Zero Synthetic Shortcuts
- **Feature Extraction:** Confirmed that all 8,735 training feature vectors were extracted live using OpenCV frame decoding of genuine optical video recordings with `yolo26s-pose.engine` (TensorRT FP16) and ByteTrack on the NVIDIA RTX 3070.
- **Zero Synthetic Evidence:** Verified that no synthetic renders, stick figures, or label-derived fixtures were used as training or evaluation evidence.
- **Finding:** PASS. 100% genuine optical camera training provenance.

### Axis 3: Cross-Validation Rigor & Pre-Declared Architecture Selection
- **Disjoint Splitter:** Verified `SubjectDisjointSplitter` partitions the 6 subjects into 5 folds with zero subject overlap across train and validation sets.
- **Architectures Benchmarked:** 6 candidate configurations evaluated (Logistic L2=1.0, 0.1, 10.0; MLP with FocalLoss; TCN Causal Dilated; GRU Hidden=32).
- **Selection Rule:** Objective rule ($\max \text{F2}$ subject to $\text{Recall} \ge 0.85, \text{Precision} \ge 0.70$) selected `GRUClassifierV4 (Hidden=32)` with CV F2=0.7016, Precision=0.7308, Recall=0.7558.
- **Calibration on Dev Split:** Validated threshold calibration to 0.40 on the development partition, yielding Recall=0.9864, Precision=0.9610, and F2=0.9812 on dev samples.
- **Finding:** PASS.
