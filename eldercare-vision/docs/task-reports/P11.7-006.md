# P11.7-006 — Task Report: Real Multi-Source Dataset Acquisition & Ingestion Protocol

## 1. Summary

- **Task ID:** `P11.7-006`
- **Objective:** Formulate and implement the V4 real multi-source dataset acquisition & ingestion protocol under the governance established in P11.7-004. Ingest legally cleared genuine optical camera datasets, compute cryptographic SHA-256 digests, extract video metadata, enforce optical authenticity validation (detecting and barring synthetic renders), parse temporal fall annotations, compile a unified master V4 manifest (`datasets/manifests/v4_multi_source_manifest.json` and `.csv`), and verify subject-disjoint split isolation under `DatasetSplitGuard`.
- **Status:** **COMPLETE**
- **Outcome:** Successfully ingested all 70 raw optical camera sequences from the UR Fall Detection Dataset (11,936 frames, 397.86 seconds of genuine optical capture). Built the automated `DatasetIngestionEngine` with `OpticalAuthenticityValidator` to quantitatively distinguish genuine optical sensor captures from flat synthetic renders. Cryptographically hashed every video with SHA-256 and aligned ground-truth temporal intervals (`fall_start_frame`, `fall_end_frame`, `lying_start_frame`). Verified zero subject overlap between dev (`subj-01`..`06`) and test (`subj-07`..`10`) via `DatasetSplitGuard`. Produced master manifests and a comprehensive audit report.

## 4. Key Governance & Integrity Enforcements

1. **Optical Authenticity Discrimination:**
   - Real URFD videos scored average unique colors $> 2,800$ and Laplacian variance $> 600$, confirming authentic optical sensor grain and surface texture. All 70 videos received `deployment_evidence=true`.
   - Quarantined legacy UP-Fall animations failed authenticity (unique colors $< 400$, flat monochrome planes, zero sensor noise) and are barred from `deployment_evidence=true`.
2. **Subject Disjointness:**
   - $\text{Subjects}(\text{Dev}) \cap \text{Subjects}(\text{Test}) = \emptyset$.
   - Confirmed by `DatasetSplitGuard.verify_partitions`.
3. **Zero Duplicate Video Hashes:**
   - 70 unique SHA-256 digests verified bit-for-bit against disk.
4. **Programmatic Holdout Enforcement:**
   - Access to `test` or `holdout` during training routines raises `HoldoutAccessError`.
5. **Ground-Truth Temporal Interval Alignment:**
   - Ground truth fall intervals (`fall_start_frame`, `fall_end_frame`, `lying_start_frame`) parsed from official `urfall-cam0-falls.csv` across all 30 fall sequences.
