# P11.7-010 — Task Report: Real-World Data Augmentation

## 1. Summary

- **Task ID:** `P11.7-010`
- **Objective:** Apply real-world physical and kinematic track perturbations (speed variation $\pm 25\%$, keypoint occlusions, tracking gap injection, camera tilt perturbations) to development split tracks to expand robustness against edge cases without synthetic shortcuts. Retrain `GRUClassifierV4`, evaluate on perturbation stress-test benchmark, and freeze hardened weights to `models/temporal_fall_classifier_v4.json`.
- **Status:** **COMPLETE**
- **Outcome:** Successfully constructed augmented development dataset (`datasets/cache/v4_dev_augmented_features.npz`) containing 16,949 samples (5,495 falls, 11,454 ADLs) via multi-modal perturbations applied exclusively to the 42 dev videos. Retrained `GRUClassifierV4` on the augmented partition. On a severe perturbation stress test, the hardened model achieved Recall=**99.73%** (vs 91.08% baseline, +8.65%), Precision=**88.82%** (vs 87.27% baseline), and F2=**0.9734** (vs 0.9029 baseline, +7.05%). Updated frozen model `models/temporal_fall_classifier_v4.json` (SHA-256: `c791616840d840742a64fb9465e3d30ae99b6d38fcb93542934720b71e450911`) and published formal report `docs/reports/P11.7-010-data-augmentation-report.md`.
