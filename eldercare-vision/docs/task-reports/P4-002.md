## 2. Invariant & Feature Verification

- **Geometry Features:** Extracts body center, bounding box dimensions, aspect ratio ($H/W$), torso angle in degrees ($[0^\circ, 90^\circ]$ where $90^\circ$ is upright and $0^\circ$ is horizontal), centroid height, and mean keypoint confidence.
- **Robust Fallback:** When keypoints are missing (e.g. occluded lower limbs), gracefully falls back to bounding box geometry and available keypoint subsets without raising exceptions.
- **Temporal Dynamics:** Computes vertical displacement ($\Delta y$), vertical velocity, peak instantaneous downward vertical velocity, height-normalized velocity, aspect ratio rate of change, torso angle change, and post-fall stillness metric.
- **ADL vs Fall Discrimination:** Walking and sitting exhibit normalized downward velocity $\approx 0$, while rapid fall descent triggers peak normalized velocity $> 1.0$ heights/s and negative aspect ratio / angle rate of change.
- **Multi-Person Isolation:** Features are extracted per `(camera_id, track_id)` stream without cross-track interference.
- **Framework Freedom:** Pure standard library and math implementation. Zero dependencies on `torch` or `ultralytics`.
