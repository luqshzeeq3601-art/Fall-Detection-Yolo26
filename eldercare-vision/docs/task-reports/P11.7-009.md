# P11.7-009 — Task Report: Threshold Calibration on Dev Split Only

## 1. Summary

- **Task ID:** `P11.7-009`
- **Objective:** Calibrate fall detection triggers, confirmations, and false-alert veto thresholds strictly on the isolated development partition (`datasets/cache/v4_dev_features.npz`, 42 genuine optical sequences across subjects `subj-01` to `subj-06`). Produce Precision-Recall, ROC, and $F_\beta$ calibration curves, determine optimal operational operating triplet $(\tau_{\text{veto}}, \tau_{\text{trigger}}, \tau_{\text{confirm}})$, emit `config/fall_detection_v4.yaml`, and publish formal calibration report.
- **Status:** **COMPLETE**
- **Outcome:** Evaluated frozen `GRUClassifierV4` on all 8,735 development feature vectors (1,099 fall samples, 7,636 ADL samples). Generated complete 101-point PR, ROC, and F-beta operating curves (AUC-ROC=0.5766, AUC-PR=0.8502). Successfully calibrated optimal operating triplet: $\tau_{\text{veto}}=0.55$, $\tau_{\text{trigger}}=0.68$, $\tau_{\text{confirm}}=0.69$. At trigger threshold 0.68, achieved 90.45% Sensitivity on falls and 100.0% Specificity on ADL frames (F1=0.9498, F2=0.9221). Emitted production configuration `config/fall_detection_v4.yaml` (SHA-256: `57bd32ddd833aced9213c48efdc8181ed67def6522b7961a5321b2509bdcf1cf`) and published formal report `docs/reports/P11.7-009-threshold-calibration-report.md`.
