3. **Routers (`src/eldercare/api/routers/`):**
   - `health.py`: `GET /health` (liveness: 200 OK) and `GET /ready` (readiness: probes DB connectivity `SELECT 1`, returning 503 on database outage).

   - `cameras.py`: `GET /cameras`, `GET /cameras/{camera_id}`, `POST /cameras`, `PATCH /cameras/{camera_id}`. RTSP URLs and credentials strictly excluded.
